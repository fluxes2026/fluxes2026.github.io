Short program of the conference and the school

  • 12–13.08 – Conference "Greenhouse Gas Fluxes: Key Trends in Modern Research" (IEE RAS)
  • 14.08 – Transfer by bus of Conference and School participants to the Central Forest Reserve (IEE RAS → CFSR, 6h)
  • 15.08 – Field seminar in the Reserve with an excursion to four eddy covariance sites and to the ecological trail through typical southern taiga landscapes of the Reserve. (CFSR)
  • 16.08 – Transfer by bus to Moscow for Conference participants not staying for the field school. (CFSR → MSK)
  • 16.08–19.08 – School participants stay in the Reserve attending lectures, seminars, and conducting field research (CFSR)
  • 20.08 – Transfer by bus of School participants to Moscow (CFSR → MSK)

    • IEE RAS: A.N. Severtsov Institute of Ecology and Evolution of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 33
    CFSR: Central Forest State Reserve, Tver Region, Nelidovsky District, Zapovedny settlement
    MSK: Moscow, Russia

Participation in the Conference 12–13.08 is possible is possible in person or on-line.
The summer field school 14–20.08 is on-site only event.
